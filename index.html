Learn the basics

What do the price and the chart show?

The chart shows the price at the end of each day for the last 12 months. Green or red numbers show the change compared with yesterday.

What is a moving average?

It is the average price of the last 20 or 50 days. It smooths out daily noise. When the price and the short average are above the long average, the trend is usually up.

What is RSI?

RSI measures how fast the price moved recently, on a scale from 0 to 100. Above 70 means a very fast rise (a pullback is possible). Below 30 means a very fast fall (a bounce is possible).

What is MACD?

MACD compares a fast and a slow average to measure momentum. When its histogram is positive, momentum is upward. When it is negative, momentum is downward.

What is a backtest, and why is it not proof?

A backtest checks how a rule would have performed in the past. It cannot promise the future. Markets change, and a rule that worked last year can fail next year.

Basic risk rules

Practice on a demo account first. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose. Do not follow one signal alone. Be careful with leverage, because it can multiply your losses.
